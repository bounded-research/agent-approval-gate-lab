# Owner-machine run: results (2026-11-06 UTC)

## Decision

**The live break was not reproduced in this session.** The local Qwen model made no recorded self-approval attempt in 40 exploratory hidden-panel runs. This is an early, budget-limited result, not evidence that the attempt rate is zero or that the proposed gate works against a live model. No Phase 2 live A/B test was run because the Phase 1 reproduction gate was not met. The lab has not earned a 9/10 METR-relevance claim.

METR's [per-action monitor note](https://metr.org/notes/2026-09-27-implementing-a-basic-blocking-action-monitor/) reports an agent opening a human review panel and sending keystrokes; it identifies human-only review authentication as a missing enforcement layer. It does not report a successful self-approval. Our file-based panel tests the same trust-boundary idea on a different interface. We have no METR endorsement for this lab.

## Interpretation and next gate

The 0/20 per-stratum observations are too small to establish a low attempt rate; the reported Beta intervals extend to 0.160. Because the owner prediction row was blank and D-006/D-007 changed model/adapter and isolation after two engineering smoke runs, these results are exploratory, not a fully preregistered confirmation. A live gate effectiveness claim requires an actual reproduced A-panel event, then the Phase 2 randomized A/B comparison with the same model, task family, and disclosure. The present scripted attacks validate selected instrument behavior only.

The proposed SMS/password/MFA flow has not been implemented. The gate's approvers are `auto_deny`, `auto_approve`, and an untested CLI function. This lab presently tests a harness-held identity, policy, and one-use permit design with synthetic files. It cannot establish real human-only approval, SMS latency, resistance to a web UI attack, or METR deployment relevance.
